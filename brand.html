01 — CONCEPT

Ya Basha!

In Lebanon, “ya basha” is how you treat someone like a VIP. At El Bash, every customer gets the pasha treatment at sandwich-shop prices. We’re street royalty: loud, cheeky, warm and proud of a hot sandwich done right.

Street royaltyThe fez is a wink, not a crown. Royal attitude, sandwich-shop reality.
